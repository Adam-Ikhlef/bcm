# Code of conduct

Be kind and constructive. Disagree with ideas, not people.

- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks
- Keep discussions in English so everyone can follow
- Assume good intent; explain, do not mock
- Do not share other people's private information

Maintainers may edit, close or remove anything that breaks these rules and may block repeat offenders.
Report conduct problems to the maintainers privately (contact details are on the maintainers' GitHub profiles).
